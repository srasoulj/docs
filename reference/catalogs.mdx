Alongside the per-venue menus, Rumizi runs two public pages that answer
general questions rather than menu questions:

They live at [rumizi.com/drinks](https://rumizi.com/drinks) and
[rumizi.com/steaks](https://rumizi.com/steaks). Anyone can use them — your
restaurant doesn't need to be on Rumizi, and there's nothing to sign in to.

## What they know

These assistants don't read any one venue's menu. They answer from a
**platform-wide catalog** built from what appears across Rumizi menus in
aggregate — which drinks and cuts are common, what they're called, how
they're described.

That aggregation is anonymous. Nothing traces back to a particular
restaurant, and no venue's prices or menu are exposed through them.

## Per-product pages

Below each assistant is a **Popular drinks / Popular steaks on Rumizi
menus** section. Each entry has its own page with:

- **At a glance** — the basic facts.
- **Tasting notes** (drinks) or **Flavour notes** (steaks).
- **Food pairings** on a drink; **Pairings** on a cut.
- **How to serve it**.
- **On Rumizi menus** — how many restaurants carry it and a typical price
  range, aggregated anonymously. No venue is named or linked.
- **Also listed as** — the other names menus use for the same thing.
- **Related drinks / Related cuts**.
- **Ask about this drink / cut** — jumps into the chat with it in context.

## How the chat behaves

The same chat you get at a table, minus anything venue-specific:

- **Voice** — a mic on an empty composer, a send arrow once you've typed.
- **Starter chips** — four suggested questions to open with, then
  follow-ups the assistant generates from its own answers.
- **Stop** — a control under the typing indicator to cut a long answer
  short. Stopping isn't an error; your question stays put.
- **Retry** — a failed answer keeps your message so you can try again.
- **Clear chat** — asks first, then wipes the conversation from your
  browser and starts fresh.

Your conversation is kept in your own browser, capped at the most recent
few dozen messages. It isn't tied to an account, because there isn't one.

## For vendors

Nothing here needs setting up and nothing is billed to you. If your menu
lists a wine or a cut that shows up commonly across Rumizi, it may
contribute anonymously to the catalog — but these pages never link to,
name, or price an individual venue.
