[rumizi.com/restaurants](https://rumizi.com/restaurants) is a public page
listing every restaurant and café running on Rumizi, grouped by city. It's
open to anyone, indexed by search engines, and needs no account.

## How it's organised

Venues are grouped into **city sections**, cities in A→Z order, with a jump
bar to skip between them. Venues that haven't filled in a city fall into a
**More places** section pinned at the end.

<Tip>
If your venue is landing in "More places", set your city in **Settings**.
That one field moves you into a real section where people actually look.
</Tip>

## What your card shows

| On the card | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| Hero image | **Settings → Brand**. Without one you get a plain monogram of your venue's first letter. |
| Logo | **Settings → Brand**. |
| Venue name | Your brand name. |
| City | **Settings**. Also decides which section you appear in. |
| Short description | **Settings**. |

Tapping a card opens that venue's Rumizi page.

Setting a hero image is the single highest-value thing you can do here — a
monogram card next to a photo of someone's dining room doesn't get the tap.
See [Brand images](/vendors/venue/brand).

<Note>
The directory refreshes about once an hour, so a brand-new venue or a
freshly uploaded photo may take a little while to appear. Nothing is
wrong — give it an hour.
</Note>

## Short links for printed QR codes

Rumizi can hand out short links of the form `rumizi.com/go/abc123` that
redirect to a venue page. They exist for print: a short code is easier to
scan reliably at small sizes and easier to read aloud than a full URL.

Because the redirect is resolved by Rumizi rather than baked into the code,
a short link can carry campaign tags — so a QR on a table tent and one on a
flyer can be told apart in your stats without printing two different
destinations.

<Warning>
Print the address you were given. Don't hand-build a QR code out of a
guessed short link — if the code doesn't resolve, the tables it's printed
on go dead, and that's an expensive reprint.
</Warning>
