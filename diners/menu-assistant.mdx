Rumizi has a built-in chat assistant that reads the same menu you see.
Use it when there's too much to scroll through, when you have dietary
questions, or when you just can't decide.

## Where to find it

At most venues the assistant **is** the first thing you see — scanning the
QR code opens the chat, with the browsable menu one tap away in the header.
From inside the menu, the chat icon in the header brings it back.

On a dish's detail page you don't open the chat at all: tap one of the
**question chips** under the dish and the answer arrives in the
conversation. See [Question chips](#question-chips-on-a-dish) below.

## Question chips on a dish

Open any dish — from the menu, or from a card the assistant showed you —
and you'll find three or four ready-made questions built from that dish:

- *"What's in it?"*
- *"Without garlic?"* — using an ingredient the dish actually has
- *"Contains nuts?"* — using an allergen the dish actually lists
- *"What goes with it?"*

Tap one and it's sent as a question about that dish by name, so the answer
comes back about the right thing without you typing anything.

## Talk to it instead of typing

The composer has a **mic**. Tap it, say your question, tap again to stop —
and it sends straight away, no re-reading and re-tapping. Recording stops
on its own after a minute.

It works in nine of the ten languages Rumizi supports. In Punjabi it still
works, it just figures out the language from what it hears instead of being
told.

## Guided discovery

If you're not sure what to ask, the assistant walks you through it. After
the opening questions, it asks one short thing at a time — *"Craving
something warming or something fresh?"* — and each answer narrows the
menu down. In two or three questions you land on 3–4 recommended dishes,
shown as cards you can tap.

You can always type a question in plain language instead — the guided
questions are a starting point, not a fixed script.

## The assistant remembers what you say

Within a single venue, the assistant remembers what you told it you like
or don't like ("I hate cilantro", "big fan of miso") and factors it into
later answers. When it saves something, a small **Noted: … · Undo** row
appears above the follow-up chips — tap **Undo** to drop it. Your tastes
are scoped to this venue only; they don't follow you to other Rumizi
restaurants.

Marking a dish **Not for me** feeds the same thing from the other
direction — the assistant stops suggesting it. That one you can apply just
at this restaurant or everywhere. See
[Scan and browse](/diners/scan-and-browse#not-for-me).

## What the assistant does not do

- **Take your order.** Rumizi is browse-and-ask; the assistant helps you
  decide, then you order with your server the way you normally would.
- **Charge anything.** Rumizi's diner flow doesn't take payments.
- **See other tables.** Each session is scoped to the venue you're at.

## Clearing and starting over

Tap **Clear chat** to wipe the conversation history on your phone.

## A word on accuracy

The assistant reads the menu as the venue entered it. It's usually
right, but it's still AI, so for **allergen or dietary questions that
matter to your health, always confirm with your server**. I'd rather you
ask twice than end up with something you can't eat.
