Rumizi has a built-in chat assistant that reads the same menu you see. Use
it when there's too much to scroll through, when you have dietary
questions, or when you just can't decide.

## Where to find it

On a Rumizi menu, tap the **chat icon** in the header. A chat panel
slides in over the menu. You can close it at any time and your cart
stays where it was.

## A quick hello, first

On your first visit to a venue's chat, the assistant asks a few short,
tappable questions — is it your first time here, roughly where are you
from, and what language you'd like to chat in. It uses those answers to
open with a short personalized tour of the menu.

Your answers are remembered on your phone for that venue, so you're
never asked again. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

## What you can ask

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="What's vegetarian?" icon="leaf">
    *"Which of the starters are vegetarian?"* The assistant only
    suggests items from the actual menu.
  </Card>
  <Card title="What's spicy?" icon="pepper-hot">
    *"Something spicy but not too heavy?"* It reads item descriptions
    and tags.
  </Card>
  <Card title="What goes well with the pizza?" icon="wine-glass">
    *"What drinks pair with the Margherita?"* It knows the venue's
    cross-sell suggestions and can suggest AI pairings too.
  </Card>
  <Card title="I have an allergy" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    *"I'm allergic to nuts. What's safe?"* The assistant reads allergen
    info but always tells you to double-check with your server for
    anything critical.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Talking to it with your voice

The composer's main input is a microphone. Tap it, speak, and Rumizi
sends what you said as soon as you stop talking — no separate send tap.
If you'd rather type, start typing and the mic turns back into a send
arrow.

<Tip>
Voice works well when the venue is loud and scrolling with one hand is
awkward. If your device can't record audio, the composer falls back to
the classic send button.
</Tip>

## Cards, carousels, and item pop-ups

When the assistant suggests dishes, they appear as a horizontal row of
cards — one card per item, same shape every time. Tap a card and an
item pop-up opens **over** the chat with the photo, description, tags,
ingredients, allergens, and an **Add to order** button. Close it and
you're right back in the conversation, exactly where you left off.

For broader questions like *"what does this restaurant serve?"* the
reply is broken into small sections with a heading, a one-line blurb,
and a card row per section. Tap **See all** on a section to drill down.
Dish or category names inside the assistant's prose are tappable too —
a dish opens the pop-up, a category asks the assistant for more.

## Tappable ingredients

If an ingredient in the assistant's reply — or in an item pop-up — is
underlined, you can tap it to see a short description in your language
and a few reference photos. Useful when the menu mentions something
you've never met before (*sumac*, *nduja*, *chinotto*).

## Building a cart by chat

You can also ask the assistant to add things to your cart:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe what you want">
    *"Add one Margherita, one Caesar salad, and two sparkling waters."*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the proposed change">
    The assistant shows you exactly what it's about to add — names,
    quantities, and any modifiers. Nothing touches your cart until you
    confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Apply">
    On confirmation, the items land in your cart. You can keep editing
    from chat or switch back to the normal menu view.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The assistant never adds, removes, or changes anything in your cart
without an explicit **Apply** tap from you.
</Note>

## Telling it what you don't like

On any item — in the assistant's cards, in the pop-up, or on the menu
— you can tap **Not for me**. The dish dims on the menu, and the
assistant follows up once to ask *why* (too spicy, don't like the
ingredient, tried it before, and so on). Whatever you say steers future
recommendations away from similar dishes, and the same preferences ride
along the next time you scan a Rumizi menu somewhere else.

You can change your mind any time — tap the same button to bring the
dish back.

## Clearing and starting over

**Clear chat** has its own icon in the chat header. Tap it to wipe the
conversation history on your phone. It doesn't clear your cart or your
"not for me" preferences — only the chat messages.

## What the assistant can't do

- **Order food for you.** It builds the cart, but the final
  hand-off to the kitchen is up to you and your server.
- **Charge anything.** Rumizi's diner flow doesn't take payments.
- **See other tables.** Each assistant session is scoped to the venue
  you're at and the cart on your phone.

## A word on accuracy

The assistant reads the menu as the venue entered it. It's usually
right, but it's still AI, so for **allergen or dietary questions that
matter to your health, always confirm with your server**. I'd rather you
ask twice than end up with something you can't eat.
