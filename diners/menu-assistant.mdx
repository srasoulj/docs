Rumizi has a built-in chat assistant that reads the same menu you see. Use
it when there's too much to scroll through, when you have dietary
questions, or when you just can't decide.

## Where to find it

On a Rumizi menu, tap the **chat icon** in the header. A chat panel
slides in over the menu. You can close it at any time and your order
stays where it was.

## Quick intents

You don't have to know what to type. A row of **quick-intent chips**
sits directly above the message box in both the full-page chat and the
floating panel:

- **Best sellers** — the venue's most-ordered dishes.
- **Chef's recommendations** — what the venue wants to spotlight.
- **Surprise me** — a curated pick based on what's on the menu.

The bar stays there for the whole conversation, so you can jump between
intents without scrolling back up. It's a shortcut — anything a chip
does you can also just say in your own words.

## What you can ask

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="What's vegetarian?" icon="leaf">
    *"Which of the starters are vegetarian?"* The assistant only
    suggests items from the actual menu.
  </Card>
  <Card title="What's spicy?" icon="pepper-hot">
    *"Something spicy but not too heavy?"* It reads item descriptions
    and tags.
  </Card>
  <Card title="What goes well with the pizza?" icon="wine-glass">
    *"What drinks pair with the Margherita?"* It knows the venue's own
    cross-sell suggestions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="I have an allergy" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    *"I'm allergic to nuts. What's safe?"* The assistant reads allergen
    info but always tells you to double-check with your server for
    anything critical.
  </Card>
</Columns>

After each reply, the assistant shows **contextual follow-up chips**
under the answer — tailored to what it just said, not the same generic
prompts every time. Tap one to keep the conversation going, or ignore
them and type your own question.

## Building an order by chat

The assistant helps you build your order without leaving the chat.
There are three ways items land in your tray:

<Steps>
  <Step title="One dish recommended? Tap to add.">
    When the assistant suggests a single dish (for example, after
    *Surprise me*), the reply shows a **dish card** with a quick-add
    button. One tap puts it in your order tray.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Several dishes recommended? Swipe the deck.">
    Multi-dish recommendations render as a **swipeable card deck**.
    Swipe right (or tap the ✓) to add a dish to your tray, swipe left
    (or tap the ✗) to skip. Tap the card itself to open full details
    first. A progress indicator shows how far through the deck you are.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ordering a full round? Describe it.">
    *"Add one Margherita, one Caesar salad, and two sparkling waters."*
    The assistant shows a **confirmation card** with exactly what it's
    about to add — names, quantities, modifiers — and nothing lands
    until you tap **Apply**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Individual swipe/tap adds put dishes straight into your tray so
building an order feels fluid. Bulk changes proposed as a batch still
wait for your explicit **Apply** tap so you never get surprised.
</Note>

## Your order tray

Above the message box there's a **persistent order tray** that shows
the live count and subtotal of what you've added so far. It pulses
briefly whenever something new lands so you can see the change without
scrolling. Tap it any time to open the full order and edit quantities,
modifiers, or notes — the same order you'd see from the menu's cart
button. The tray hides itself when your order is empty.

## Clearing and starting over

Tap **Clear chat** to wipe the conversation history on your phone. It
doesn't clear your order — only the chat messages.

## What the assistant can't do

- **Order food for you.** It builds the order in your tray, but the
  final hand-off to the kitchen is up to you and your server.
- **Charge anything.** Rumizi's diner flow doesn't take payments.
- **See other tables.** Each assistant session is scoped to the venue
  you're at and the order on your phone.

## A word on accuracy

The assistant reads the menu as the venue entered it. It's usually
right, but it's still AI, so for **allergen or dietary questions that
matter to your health, always confirm with your server**. I'd rather you
ask twice than end up with something you can't eat.
