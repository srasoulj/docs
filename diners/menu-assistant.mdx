Rumizi has a built-in chat assistant that reads the same menu you see. Use
it when there's too much to scroll through, when you have dietary
questions, or when you just can't decide.

## Where to find it

On a Rumizi menu, tap the **chat icon** in the header. A chat panel
slides in over the menu. You can close it at any time and your cart
stays where it was.

The chat surface has a subtle fine-dining backdrop — a quiet pattern of
place-setting marks — so it feels like part of the venue, not a generic
text window. Assistant replies appear in full as soon as they're ready;
nothing animates character-by-character.

## Getting started: suggestion chips

You don't have to know what to type. After the welcome message, and any
time you're between turns, the assistant shows a row of **tappable
chips** with icons — *What's popular?*, *Something light*, *Vegetarian?*,
and so on. Tap one and it sends that question as if you'd typed it. The
chips are also localized to the venue's language.

## Multi-menu venues

If a venue has more than one menu (for example **Brunch**, **Dinner**,
**Wine**, **Desserts**), the assistant opens with a short, skippable
flow so you land on the right one quickly:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a menu, or answer a couple of questions">
    The first screen shows a tidy grid of every menu currently being
    served (with next-opening badges on the ones that aren't). Tap a
    menu to focus the conversation on it, or answer 2–3 quick questions
    (party size, food vs drinks, dietary needs) and the assistant
    suggests the best fit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse all menus if you want the full list">
    Tap **Browse all menus** for a searchable popup grouped by what's
    being served now vs later. Useful at venues with many sections.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch any time">
    Once you're chatting, you can still say *"What about the wine
    list?"* and the assistant will move with you. It biases toward the
    menu you're focused on but can answer across all of them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you can ask

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="What's vegetarian?" icon="leaf">
    *"Which of the starters are vegetarian?"* The assistant only
    suggests items from the actual menu.
  </Card>
  <Card title="What's spicy?" icon="pepper-hot">
    *"Something spicy but not too heavy?"* It reads item descriptions
    and tags.
  </Card>
  <Card title="What goes well with the pizza?" icon="wine-glass">
    *"What drinks pair with the Margherita?"* It knows the venue's own
    cross-sell suggestions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="I have an allergy" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    *"I'm allergic to nuts. What's safe?"* The assistant reads allergen
    info but always tells you to double-check with your server for
    anything critical.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A quick overview" icon="list">
    *"Standout picks from the Wine menu?"* The wording adapts to the
    menu — you'll get **picks** rather than "dishes" when you're on a
    drinks or dessert list.

  <Card title="Compare across menus" icon="layers">
    *"What from the dessert menu would pair with this?"* The assistant
    can reach into other menus at the venue without you switching tabs.
  </Card>

## How replies look

When the assistant recommends items, you see them as cards you can tap
to open the full item page or add to your cart:

- **One result** renders as a single full-width **hero card** — bigger
  photo, full description, primary action right there.
- **Two or more results** render as a swipeable **carousel** of compact
  cards so you can scan side-by-side without scrolling away from the
  chat.

Out-of-stock items appear in the cards too, so the assistant can still
talk about them, but the add-to-cart action is disabled until the venue
flips them back on.

## Building a cart by chat

You can also ask the assistant to add things to your cart:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe what you want">
    *"Add one Margherita, one Caesar salad, and two sparkling waters."*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the proposed change">
    The assistant shows you exactly what it's about to add — names,
    quantities, and any modifiers. Nothing touches your cart until you
    confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Apply">
    On confirmation, the items land in your cart. You can keep editing
    from chat or switch back to the normal menu view.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The assistant never adds, removes, or changes anything in your cart
without an explicit **Apply** tap from you.
</Note>

## Clearing and starting over

Tap **Clear chat** to wipe the conversation history on your phone. It
doesn't clear your cart — only the chat messages.

## What the assistant can't do

- **Order food for you.** It builds the cart, but the final
  hand-off to the kitchen is up to you and your server.
- **Charge anything.** Rumizi's diner flow doesn't take payments.
- **See other tables.** Each assistant session is scoped to the venue
  you're at and the cart on your phone.

## A word on accuracy

The assistant reads the menu as the venue entered it. It's usually
right, but it's still AI, so for **allergen or dietary questions that
matter to your health, always confirm with your server**. I'd rather you
ask twice than end up with something you can't eat.
