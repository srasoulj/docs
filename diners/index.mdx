## The short version

<Steps>
  <Step title="Scan the QR code">
    Point your phone's camera at the code on the table. Tap the link
    that pops up — no app required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer a couple of quick taps">
    The chat opens and asks whether you've been before, where you're
    visiting from, and what language you'd like. Tap the pills, or skip
    the whole thing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask, or browse">
    Ask the assistant what's good — by typing, or by tapping the mic and
    saying it. Or open the full menu from the header and scroll the
    categories yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dig into a dish">
    Tap a dish for photos, price, pairings, and tappable ingredients.
    Ready-made question chips let you ask about it in one tap.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order with your server">
    Once you've picked what you want, order the normal way. Rumizi
    doesn't take orders; it just helps you decide.
  </Step>
</Steps>
