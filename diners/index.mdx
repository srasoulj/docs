## The short version

<Steps>
  <Step title="Scan the QR code">
    Point your phone's camera at the code on the table. Tap the link
    that pops up — no app required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer a few quick questions">
    A welcome card asks 3–4 quick things about what you're in the mood
    for. Tap answers as chips, or skip it entirely.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse the menu">
    Scroll categories, tap a dish for details, photos, and pairings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask questions">
    Tap the assistant icon in the header — or **Ask AI about this item**
    on any dish — to ask about allergens, spice level, or
    recommendations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order with your server">
    Once you've picked what you want, order the normal way. Rumizi
    doesn't take orders; it just helps you decide.
  </Step>
</Steps>
