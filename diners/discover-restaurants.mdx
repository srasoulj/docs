Not at a table yet? The Rumizi directory lists every restaurant and café
that's live on the platform, so you can find one nearby or browse a
menu before you go.

## Open the directory

Go to [rumizi.com/restaurants](https://rumizi.com/restaurants). No sign-in,
no app — it opens in your browser like any other web page.

## What you'll see

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Grouped by city" icon="map">
    Restaurants are bucketed by their city. If you're in a place with
    lots of listings, use the sticky city nav at the top to jump to your
    city in one tap.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Instant search" icon="search">
    The search box filters by restaurant name **or** city as you type.
    Clear it with the **X** to see everything again.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A card per restaurant" icon="utensils">
    Each card shows the venue name, city, and a hero image (if the
    restaurant added one). Tap a card to open that restaurant's public
    menu.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Only live venues" icon="check">
    The directory shows venues that are active, not soft-deleted, and
    have a published menu. If a place isn't there yet, it may not be
    fully set up.
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Tip>
Missing city? Restaurants that haven't filled in a city are grouped under
**More places** at the bottom of the page.
</Tip>
