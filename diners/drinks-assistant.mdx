Rumizi's drinks assistant answers questions about **wines, beers, spirits,
and cocktails** using general knowledge of standard market products. Unlike
the [menu assistant](/diners/menu-assistant), it isn't tied to a specific
restaurant — you can use it any time, whether the venue you're at runs on
Rumizi or not.

## Where to find it

Go to [rumizi.com/drinks](https://rumizi.com/drinks). The page is public
and opens in the browser — no sign-in, no app.

## Voice input

Tap the microphone in the composer to speak your question. Rumizi
transcribes what you said and drops the text into the input; you review and
send it. See [Voice input](/diners/menu-assistant#voice-input) for the
details that apply on every assistant.

## What it can't do

- **Order anything.** The drinks assistant is a knowledge tool. Ordering
  happens on the venue's own menu.
- **Quote real prices.** When it references what dishes or drinks cost, it
  gives anonymous price ranges built from Rumizi's aggregated catalog — it
  never names a specific venue.
- **Speak for a specific restaurant.** If you want to know whether *this*
  bar has a Negroni, open their menu and use the [menu assistant](/diners/menu-assistant).

## Accuracy

The assistant reads from general knowledge and Rumizi's platform-wide
drinks catalog. It's useful for orientation, not a substitute for a
sommelier or bartender. **Please drink responsibly** — the disclaimer on
the page says the same thing.
