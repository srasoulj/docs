You don't install anything to use a Rumizi menu. If the restaurant has a
QR code on the table, your phone already has everything it needs.

## Open the menu

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your camera">
    Use the built-in camera app on iPhone or Android. No special QR
    scanner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Point it at the QR code">
    Hold the camera over the code for a second. A link appears at the
    top (on iPhone) or as a notification (on Android).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the link">
    The menu opens in your browser. That's the whole setup.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Can't scan? Ask your server for the venue's Rumizi link. It's just
`rumizi.com/venue-name`.
</Tip>

## First visit — a quick welcome

The first time you open a Rumizi menu, a small welcome card asks a few
quick questions — usually 3 or 4 — like what you're in the mood for,
any dishes to avoid, and how spicy you like things. Tap an answer chip
per question, or hit **Skip all** to jump straight to the menu. Your
answers help the assistant give better recommendations later.

## Find what you want

The menu is organised in categories — *Starters*, *Mains*, *Drinks*, and
so on. A few things you can do to find dishes faster:

- **Scroll the Popular row at the top.** Venues pin their best dishes
  here.
- **Tap a tag filter** (Vegetarian, Spicy, Gluten-free) to narrow the
  menu down.
- **Search.** The search box matches item names and ingredients.
- **Switch language** from the header if the default isn't yours. See
  [Languages](/diners/languages).

## Item detail

Tap any dish to open its detail page. You'll see:

- A photo (if the venue added one).
- The full description and price.
- Any allergen or dietary tags.
- **Tappable ingredients** — tap an ingredient to see a short
  explanation and two AI-drawn illustrations of what it is. Useful when
  you're not sure what "cotto" or "burrata" means.
- **Goes well with** — the venue's suggestions for what pairs with the
  dish. Grouped by kind (drinks, sides, desserts) so it's easy to scan.
- **Ask AI about this item** — opens the menu assistant with the item
  in context. Ask anything: allergens, spice level, how it's cooked,
  what pairs with it.

## Out of stock items

If a dish is dimmed and marked "Unavailable", the kitchen is out.
Tapping it still shows the detail page so you can see what it is.

## Ordering

Rumizi's menu is **browse-and-ask** — you use it to decide, then order
with your server the way you normally would. There's no cart, no
"send order" button, no digital ordering. Once you've picked what you
want, put the phone down and tell your server (or hold up the item on
your screen).
