Quick answers for diners. If something isn't covered, ask your server —
they have direct support from Rumizi.

## The basics

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to install an app?">
    No. Rumizi opens in your phone's browser when you scan the QR code.
    There's nothing to install.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need an account?">
    No. Browsing the menu and talking to the assistant both work without
    signing in. Some venues offer an optional account for community
    features — see [Accounts](/diners/accounts).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a cost?">
    No. Rumizi is free for diners. The restaurant pays for its own
    menu; you just use it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does it work offline?">
    You need an internet connection to load the menu and to chat with
    the assistant. Once a page is loaded it stays readable, but new
    questions and searches need the connection to be back.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Using the menu

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I actually place an order?">
    You order with your server the way you normally would. Rumizi is
    browse-and-ask — it helps you decide what to get, then the
    hand-off is human. There's no cart and no "send order" button.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I change the language?">
    Tap the language button in the header. You'll see every language
    the venue supports. Details: [Switching language](/diners/languages).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I switch to dark mode?">
    Tap the theme toggle in the header (sun / moon icon). Your choice
    is remembered on your phone for that venue.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An item looks dimmed.">
    It's marked out of stock by the venue. The item is still shown so
    you know what it normally is; the kitchen just doesn't have it
    right now.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What are those two illustrations on ingredient popups?">
    When you tap an ingredient chip on the item detail page, Rumizi shows
    a short explanation and two AI-drawn illustrations of what the
    ingredient looks like — they appear one at a time as each is drawn.
    Useful for words like "burrata", "guanciale", or "sumac" if you're not
    sure what they are. The popup title stays in the menu's own wording
    (what you'd say to your server); only the explanation is translated.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did it open a chat instead of the menu?">
    At most venues the chat is the front door — it knows the menu and
    can answer faster than scrolling. The full browsable menu is one tap
    away in the header, or at `rumizi.com/venue-name/menu`.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I filter to just the vegetarian dishes?">
    Not with a filter button — tags like *Vegetarian* are labels on the
    dishes, not filters. Use the search box, or just ask the assistant
    "which of these are vegetarian?" and it'll list them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does Not for me do?">
    It tells Rumizi to show you fewer dishes like that one — it drops
    out of the Popular row and the assistant stops suggesting it. You
    choose whether it applies just at that restaurant or everywhere. The
    venue sees anonymous counts, never who marked what.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## The menu assistant

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is the assistant answering from the actual menu?">
    Yes — it reads the same menu you see and only suggests dishes
    that are really on it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I trust it for allergen questions?">
    For anything that affects your health, confirm with your server.
    The assistant reads the venue's own allergen info, which is usually
    right, but a human server is the safest final check.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can the assistant order for me?">
    No. The assistant helps you decide — it doesn't send anything to
    the kitchen. You order with your server the normal way.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does it remember what I like?">
    Within the same venue, yes. When you tell it something like "I
    don't like mushrooms" it remembers, and shows a small **Noted · Undo**
    row so you can drop the memory if it got it wrong. Your tastes are
    scoped to this one venue and don't follow you around.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I clear the chat?">
    Tap **Clear chat** in the assistant panel.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Privacy

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Rumizi track me?">
    Rumizi stores what you need to use the menu — your language choice,
    your theme, the tastes you've told the assistant, and (if you sign
    in) your account at a venue. It doesn't sell or share that data.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
